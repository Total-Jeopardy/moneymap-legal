Delete your Money Map account

You can delete your account and data yourself, in the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Money Map and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, loans, recurring entries, personal notes and preferences.
  • Messages saved for review, and your receipt photos.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy first, use Export Transactions in More → Settings → Data & Privacy. Copies in routine backups held by our hosting providers are removed as those backups expire.

Can't open the app?

Email sylvesterjuniortettey@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Money Map account", and we will delete it for you.

Used it without an account?

Your records are only on your phone. Use Factory Reset in More → Settings to clear them, or uninstall the app.
