Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Money Map is a personal finance app. It helps you record and understand your money across mobile money, bank and cash. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who handles it, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

Money Map is built and operated by Sylvester Junior Tettey, Accra, Ghana. For anything in this policy, write to sylvesterjuniortettey@gmail.com.

Money Map has no ads and no advertising trackers. We do not collect your contacts, location or advertising ID.

Crash and error reports

If the app hits an error or crashes, it sends a short report to VedaTrace, a logging service we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains the error text, a technical trace of where it happened, how the app last closed if it was a crash, freeze or memory shutdown, the app version and build, and the Android version. Before it leaves your phone, email addresses and any long run of digits (such as amounts, phone numbers or balances) are removed from the text. We do not attach your name, email, account details or transactions to a report, and nothing is added that identifies you. Error text is technical and is not meant to contain your records. If you are offline, reports wait in the app's private storage (a small, size-limited file) and are sent when you are back online. Reports are kept until we delete them. Google Play may also give us anonymous crash and performance statistics for the app.

Using it without an account

You can use the app offline without an account. In that mode your records stay on your phone only and nothing is sent to our servers. Features that need our servers, such as AI insights, are not available until you create an account.

AI features

Some features use AI to read or summarise your own data: logging by voice, scanning a receipt, Spending DNA, goal plans, anomaly checks and, if you turn it on, smart category suggestions. For these, the app sends the relevant text or figures to our server, which passes them to AI providers (currently Anthropic, Google and Groq) to produce the result. What is sent depends on the feature: a voice transcript, the text read from a receipt, or summaries of your spending.

Smart category suggestions are off until you turn them on in Transaction Settings. When on, and only when your own history cannot place a category, the note, the type (expense or income) and the amount of the transaction you are entering are sent. Your name, accounts, balances and other transactions are not sent. You can switch it off at any time.

AI results can be wrong. Please check them before relying on them.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase hosts accounts, sign-in, the database and private receipt storage.
  • Render hosts our server that parses messages and runs the AI features.
  • VedaTrace receives the crash and error reports described above. It is hosted on Cloudflare.
  • Anthropic, Google and Groq process the text and figures described above when you use an AI feature.
  • Google runs Google Play and, on your phone, the on-device text recognition used for receipts.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the services above to run the app, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your records, messages and receipt photos are deleted (see below). Copies held in routine backups by our hosting providers are removed as those backups expire.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to More → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored under it. You can also ask us to do it by email, or follow the steps on the delete account page. If you use the app without an account, Factory Reset in Settings clears everything from your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it too.

Your choices and rights

You can see your data in the app, export your transactions from Data & Privacy, correct anything you entered, turn off SMS detection, notifications and smart suggestions, and delete your account. You can also write to us to ask what we hold about you or to correct or delete it. We handle personal data with care and aim to follow applicable data protection law, including Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account's records are protected by access rules so one person's data cannot be read by another. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

Children

Money Map is not meant for children. Please do not use it if you are under 18 without a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where it affects what is collected, tell you in the app.
